## Business Overview

KinggEasyy Auto Repair is an independently owned automotive repair shop located in Winston-Salem, North Carolina.

The business specializes in honest, high-quality automotive repairs and focuses on providing customers with detailed communication, quality workmanship, and fair pricing.

The business currently operates primarily as a physical repair shop.

## Current Challenges

Ian spends a significant amount of time responding to repetitive customer questions through text messages.

Customers frequently ask:

- What services do you offer?
- How much will my repair cost?
- What information do you need?
- When are you available?
- Where are you located?

The website should reduce repetitive conversations by answering these common questions.

---

## Website Goals

The website should:

- Present a professional online presence
- Build customer trust
- Showcase previous repairs
- Display customer reviews
- Explain services offered
- Make contacting the business easier
- Reduce repetitive texting
- Help customers submit repair requests
- Allow customers to provide vehicle information before Ian responds
