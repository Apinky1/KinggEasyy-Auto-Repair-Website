# Business Requirements

## Project Purpose

Develop a professional website for KinggEasyy Auto Repair that improves customer communication, builds trust, and reduces time spent answering repetitive questions.

---

# Business Objectives

The website shall:

- Increase online credibility.
- Showcase completed repair work.
- Display services offered.
- Provide business contact information.
- Help customers request estimates.
- Reduce repetitive phone calls and text messages.
- Improve the customer experience.
- Increase customer conversions.

---

# Primary Business Problem

Currently, a significant amount of time is spent answering repetitive customer questions before a repair is ever scheduled.

Examples include:

- What services do you offer?
- What are your hours?
- Where are you located?
- Can you work on my vehicle?
- How do I get a quote?

The website should answer these questions before the customer contacts the shop.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

- Customers can quickly understand what the shop offers.
- Customers can easily contact the business.
- Customers can submit enough information for Ian to begin preparing an estimate.
- Customers trust the business before making first contact.
- The amount of repetitive customer communication decreases.
