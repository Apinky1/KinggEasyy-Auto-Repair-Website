# Functional Requirements

## Overview

The KinggEasyy Auto Repair website shall provide customers with an easy way to learn about the business, request services, and contact the shop.

---

# Home Page

The website shall:

- Display the business name and logo.
- Display a professional hero image.
- Include a short business introduction.
- Display quick navigation to all pages.
- Include a Call Now button.
- Include a Request Estimate button.

---

# Services

The website shall:

- Display a complete list of services.
- Provide a description for each service.
- Allow customers to easily understand what repairs are offered.

---

# Gallery

The website shall:

- Display repair photos.
- Display before-and-after photos when available.
- Support future video content.

---

# About

The website shall:

- Introduce Ian.
- Explain the company's mission.
- Explain why customers should choose KinggEasyy Auto Repair.

---

# Contact

The website shall display:

- Business phone number.
- Business email.
- Shop address.
- Business hours.
- Google Maps location.
- Social media links.

---

# Estimate Request

Customers shall be able to submit:

- Name
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Vehicle Year
- Vehicle Make
- Vehicle Model
- VIN (Optional)
- Description of the issue
- Preferred contact method

---

# Future Enhancements

Version 2 may include:

- Appointment scheduling
- Online payments
- Financing
- Customer portal
- Repair status tracking
